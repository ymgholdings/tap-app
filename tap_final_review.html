01 · Product charter

Non-negotiable product requirements

Visible by consent, not hidden by default

During onboarding, adults explicitly opt into being discoverable. Once enabled, discovery remains on without an arbitrary app-level time limit. Ghost Mode is a user-selectable switch, not the default experience.

Same-place discovery

Users within effective wireless range at the same café, theater, shop, pub, airport lounge or event must be discoverable when platform conditions allow. Do not intentionally coarse-grain away venue-level relevance.

Direct and private

Core discovery, profile exchange and chat should function without internet or tap.-owned servers. Private keys, conversations and personal associations remain under user-device control.

Adult autonomy with consent boundaries

Wink supports adult dating and romantic/sexual interest; Nod is platonic; Tap exchanges selected data; Hush is private chat. Mutual agreement governs disclosure. Repeated invitations after rejection are restricted.

Expanded discovery requirements (SDP v1.1)

Users may screen new nearby results by age, gender, race/ethnicity, political leaning, interests and Nod/Wink intention. Existing trusted contacts may bypass these ordinary filters at the user's option; blocking always takes precedence. A separate Looking For taxonomy supports chit-chat, coffee, meals, travel/layovers, gym and outdoor buddies, tutoring, language exchange, friendship, dating and user-selected adult relationship intentions (DTF, FWB, NSA and third-person connections). Orientation and desired-partner preferences are separate fields. All filters run locally, with no sensitive plaintext BLE advertising and no blanket discoverability timeouts.

Critical implementation corrections

  1. Discovery is best effort, especially in the background. iOS background advertising and scanning differ from foreground behavior. No API promises continuous arbitrary broadcast or listening in all app states.
  2. Do not assume universal automatic hotspot joining. Cross-platform network handoff may require user participation or fail due to entitlement/OS constraints. Treat it as a fallback experiment.
  3. Physical impact is a user-intent hint. Correlated accelerometer spikes and optional UWB evidence are not sufficient authentication. Require a recognized, authenticated peer and a payload confirmation.
  4. Same-place relevance is retained. Display actual nearby candidates within observed wireless range; do not claim exact venue, floor or distance unless evidence supports it. Optional venue confirmation can enrich results, with no central location database.
Gate G0: On real retail iOS and Android devices, show bidirectional offline discovery, authenticated connection, contact transfer and behavior measurements across screen-on, locked and suspended states. Record unsupported paths plainly.
04 · Threat model

Privacy without disabling proximity

Protect against

  • Radio eavesdropping and passive correlating observers
  • Impersonation, replay and downgrade attacks
  • Unwanted repeated approaches
  • Accidental sharing to the wrong device
  • Stolen-device and backup exposure
  • Operator or hosting access to user messages

Accept the honest limits

  • Nearby discovery necessarily reveals some presence
  • Bluetooth signal strength does not prove venue identity
  • Rotating app identifiers cannot guarantee unlinkability against every radio observer
  • Serverless messaging has no automatic remote delivery after separation
  • Local blocks cannot reliably stop reinstallation under a fresh identity

Minimum cryptographic protocol requirements

  • Separate long-term identity material, ephemeral discovery IDs and authenticated session keys; never broadcast a stable personal public key routinely.
  • Encrypt allowed profile attributes and exchange them only at an appropriate consent stage; no plaintext demographics or intent tags in advertisements.
  • A salted hash of age, politics, race or intent is not private matching; only claim PSI/MPC/zero-knowledge after a separately reviewed construction actually implements it.
  • Use authenticated key agreement, authenticated encryption, nonce/replay defenses, session confirmation and explicit key change handling.
  • Support hybrid classical + post-quantum key establishment as a security design candidate, e.g. X25519 + ML-KEM, subject to mobile performance, library maturity and review. Evaluate ML-DSA for signatures where justified; never advertise “quantum proof.”

Future optional Finland infrastructure

If remote functions ever become necessary, prefer Finland-based infrastructure while retaining device-held decryption keys, E2EE, minimized metadata, operator-independent cryptographic verification and independent audit. Geographic hosting does not prevent lawful access to available metadata or endpoint compromise. "No operator access" is a property to prove by system design, not by country alone.

Distribution constraints are not product ideology

Apple's guidelines prohibit some hookup-oriented and predominantly random/anonymous chat experiences; store acceptance is not guaranteed. Conduct early policy review while retaining the product's stated adult purpose. A self-entered birthdate alone must be labeled an age declaration, not verified age.

06 · Messaging

Hush: local conversation with real security

Hush provides immediate encrypted conversation within a supported local transport path. It is not advertised as reliably delivering messages over arbitrary distances without a relay. Message status must distinguish queued locally, sent to peer, delivered to peer and read (if enabled).

Security requirements

Authenticated identity binding; handshake transcript verification; forward secrecy via reviewed ratcheting construction; attachment encryption; replay resistance; key-change notification; state rollback handling; peer verification UI.

User guarantees

Messages encrypted in transit and at rest, device-only keys, no operator-held recovery key, clear backups setting, local Burn Thread control with honest deletion limits. No undelivered-message promise when peers are out of range.

If evaluating Signal's libsignal, review AGPLv3 obligations and the project's statement about unsupported third-party use. Library selection follows legal and technical due diligence.

08 · Subsequent product lines

Extensions after the core network works

Car Mode / NFC decal

Optional static sticker contains no enduring personal profile data. A live authenticated local presence check gates any personal card. Protect against copied QR codes and relays; do not encourage driver distraction.

Merchant Mode

Signed local offers, merchant-owned redemption ledger and opt-in discovery. Avoid claiming that offline signatures prevent multi-register double spending. No centralized consumer activity analytics.

Monetization and store billing are not identical to a zero-network application: app-store purchases and subscriptions ordinarily involve platform services. Define those functions as optional and distinct from private social traffic.

09 · Release plan

Stage gates, not calendar promises

0 / Native radio proof of concept 2–4 wk estimate

Retail-device BLE discovery, background behavior, iOS 26 Wi-Fi Aware experiments, Android interoperability, authentication and offline transfer. Gate: demonstrated device matrix with failures documented.

1 / Protocol and privacy core 3–5 wk

Threat model, identity and key lifecycle, protocol versioning, peer consent, encryption and replay testing. Gate: independent protocol review and attack tests.

2 / MVP mobile experience 4–6 wk

Flutter app, Tap/Nod, opt-in persistent visibility, Ghost toggle, encrypted local storage, basic Hush. Gate: end-to-end offline experience.

3 / Wink and social protections 3–5 wk

Mutual interest, polite/humorous rejection, suppression and blocking, bounded requests, accessibility. Gate: abuse simulation and UX tests.

4 / Independent review + controlled beta 4–6 wk

Radio field testing, security audit, battery measurements, policy review, crash recovery and store submission preparation. Gate: release acceptance criteria.

5 / Optional commerce and Car Mode later

Merchant dashboard, offline redemption and NFC decals only after core behavior is stable.

Estimates are directional and may overlap. Schedule resets if Wi-Fi Aware interoperability or background discovery fails the proof of concept.
